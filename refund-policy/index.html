Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective as of: 19 November 2026 · Applies to payments made to PA Tax Practitioners (Pty) Ltd, trading as Payroll On The Run, for the Payroll On The Run app and Port Alfred OnCall advertising.

In short

  • Monthly payments renew automatically every month until you cancel.
  • You can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect from the next billing month.
  • We do not give pro-rata refunds for a month that has already started.
  • Duplicate or wrong payments are refunded in full.
  • OnCall ads that we reject or do not place are refunded in full.
  • Card payments are processed by Paystack. We never see or store your full card number.

1. Who we are

PA Tax Practitioners (Pty) Ltd, trading as Payroll On The Run (“we”, “us”).

Address: 8 North Street, Port Alfred, 6170

2. What this policy covers

2.1 Payroll On The Run, version downloaded from payrollontherun.com (paid through Paystack)

  • Employee slots and add-on modules (Leave, Full Transactions, Bargaining Council and Disciplinary) are billed per month. Current prices are shown on payrollontherun.com and in the app.
  • The first active employee is free for 60 days from first install. You are not charged for that period.
  • Paystack payments renew automatically every month on the saved card, until you cancel.

2.2 Payroll On The Run, Google Play version

Subscriptions bought in the Google Play version are billed by Google Play and fall under Google Play’s refund policy. You can cancel them at any time in the Google Play Store under Payments & subscriptions.

2.3 Port Alfred OnCall advertising

  • Business listings in OnCall are free. There is nothing to pay or refund.
  • Ad placements (home page, category or sub-category) are paid per month through Paystack. Current prices are shown in the OnCall app.
  • An ad goes live only after our admin has approved it. Payment alone does not place an ad.
  • Ad placements renew automatically every month, until you cancel.

3. Cancellation

  • You may cancel at any time by e-mail (section 6). Google Play subscriptions are cancelled in Google Play.
  • Cancellation takes effect from the next billing month. You keep what you paid for until the end of the current month, and we do not charge you again.
  • Payroll On The Run: paid slots and modules stop at the end of the paid month. Your payroll records stay on your own device.
  • OnCall: your ad stays live until the end of the paid month, then comes down.

4. Refunds

  • Month already started: no pro-rata refund, also not when you cancel or reduce slots or modules part-way through a month.
  • Duplicate or wrong payments: if you were charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged after a valid cancellation, we refund the extra or wrong amount in full.
  • OnCall ads we reject or do not place: refunded in full. This includes an ad that cannot be placed, for example because the space is already full.
  • OnCall ad removed after it went live because it breaks our advertising rules: no refund.
  • Refunds are paid back to the card or account you paid with, through Paystack, within 7 business days after we approve the request. Your bank may take longer to show it.

5. Disputes and chargebacks

Please contact us first, so that we can sort out the problem quickly. If you dispute a charge with your bank (a chargeback), the service is suspended while the dispute is open.

6. How to cancel or ask for a refund

Payroll On The Run: support@payrollontherun.com
Port Alfred OnCall: oncall@patax.co.za

Please include your name, the e-mail address you paid with, the date and amount, the Paystack reference if you have it, and the reason. We reply within 2 business days.

7. Payments and card details

Card payments are processed by Paystack on Paystack’s own payment page. We never see or store your full card number. See our Privacy Policy for the payment details we do keep.

8. Your rights

This policy does not limit any rights you have under South African law, including the Consumer Protection Act and the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we update it on this page and change the “effective as of” date.

10. Contact

PA Tax Practitioners (Pty) Ltd t/a Payroll On The Run · 8 North Street, Port Alfred, 6170

support@payrollontherun.com · Contact page

Terugbetaling- en Kansellasiebeleid

Geldig soos op: 19 November 2026 · Van toepassing op betalings aan PA Tax Practitioners (Pty) Ltd, handeldrywend as Payroll On The Run, vir die Payroll On The Run-app en Port Alfred OnCall-advertensies.

Kortliks

  • Maandelikse betalings hernu outomaties elke maand totdat jy kanselleer.
  • Jy kan enige tyd kanselleer. Kansellasie geld vanaf die volgende faktuurmaand.
  • Ons betaal nie pro rata terug vir ’n maand wat reeds begin het nie.
  • Dubbele of foutiewe betalings word ten volle terugbetaal.
  • OnCall-advertensies wat ons weier of nie plaas nie, word ten volle terugbetaal.
  • Kaartbetalings word deur Paystack verwerk. Ons sien of stoor nooit jou volle kaartnommer nie.

1. Wie ons is

PA Tax Practitioners (Pty) Ltd, handeldrywend as Payroll On The Run (“ons”). Adres: 8 North Street, Port Alfred, 6170

2. Wat hierdie beleid dek

  • Payroll On The Run (weergawe van payrollontherun.com, betaal deur Paystack): werknemerplekke en byvoegmodules (Verlof, Volle Transaksies, Bedingingsraad en Dissiplinêr) word maandeliks gehef. Huidige pryse staan op die webwerf en in die app. Die eerste aktiewe werknemer is 60 dae gratis vanaf eerste installasie. Paystack-betalings hernu elke maand outomaties op die gestoorde kaart, totdat jy kanselleer.
  • Payroll On The Run (Google Play-weergawe): word deur Google Play gehef en val onder Google Play se terugbetalingsbeleid. Kanselleer enige tyd in die Google Play Store onder Betalings en intekeninge.
  • Port Alfred OnCall: besigheidslysings is gratis. Advertensieplekke (tuisblad, kategorie of subkategorie) word per maand deur Paystack betaal. ’n Advertensie gaan eers aan nadat ons admin dit goedgekeur het. Advertensieplekke hernu elke maand outomaties, totdat jy kanselleer.

3. Kansellasie

Kanselleer enige tyd per e-pos (afdeling 6), of vir Google Play in Google Play. Kansellasie geld vanaf die volgende faktuurmaand. Jy behou wat jy betaal het tot die einde van die huidige maand, en ons hef jou nie weer nie. Betaalde plekke en modules hou aan die einde van die betaalde maand op; jou betaalstaatrekords bly op jou eie toestel. ’n OnCall-advertensie bly sigbaar tot die einde van die betaalde maand.

4. Terugbetalings

  • Maand reeds begin: geen pro rata-terugbetaling nie, ook nie as jy halfpad deur die maand kanselleer of plekke of modules verminder nie.
  • Dubbele of foutiewe betalings: as jy twee keer, ’n verkeerde bedrag, of ná ’n geldige kansellasie gehef is, betaal ons die ekstra of verkeerde bedrag ten volle terug.
  • OnCall-advertensies wat ons weier of nie plaas nie: ten volle terugbetaal, ook as die plek reeds vol is.
  • OnCall-advertensie verwyder nadat dit aan was omdat dit ons advertensiereëls oortree: geen terugbetaling nie.
  • Terugbetalings gaan deur Paystack terug na die kaart of rekening waarmee jy betaal het, binne 7 werksdae nadat ons die versoek goedgekeur het. Jou bank kan langer neem om dit te wys.

5. Dispute en terugvorderings

Kontak ons asseblief eers, sodat ons die probleem vinnig kan regmaak. As jy ’n hefing by jou bank betwis (’n terugvordering), word die diens opgeskort solank die dispuut oop is.

6. Hoe om te kanselleer of terugbetaling te vra

Payroll On The Run: support@payrollontherun.com
Port Alfred OnCall: oncall@patax.co.za

Gee jou naam, die e-posadres waarmee jy betaal het, die datum en bedrag, die Paystack-verwysing as jy dit het, en die rede. Ons antwoord binne 2 werksdae.

7. Betalings en kaartbesonderhede

Kaartbetalings word deur Paystack op Paystack se eie betaalblad verwerk. Ons sien of stoor nooit jou volle kaartnommer nie. Sien ons Privaatheidsbeleid.

8. Jou regte

Hierdie beleid beperk nie enige regte wat jy volgens Suid-Afrikaanse wetgewing het nie, insluitend die Wet op Verbruikersbeskerming en die Wet op Elektroniese Kommunikasie en Transaksies.

9. Veranderinge

As ons hierdie beleid verander, werk ons dit op hierdie blad by en verander ons die “geldig soos op”-datum.
