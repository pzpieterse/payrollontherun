# Payroll On The Run – Branding Guidelines
**Version 1.0 | August 2026**

## 1. Logo
- **Primary Logo**: Use the official Play Store app icon (green “Payroll OnTheRun” wordmark with motion/speed lines).
- Preferred formats: PNG with transparent background (for web), SVG if available.
- Minimum clear space: height of the letter “P” around all sides.
- Do not stretch, rotate, or change colours of the official logo.
- Favicon: Use the same green icon (square cropped version).
- Text fallback (if logo image unavailable): “Payroll On The Run” in bold, colour #0D47A1 or #2E7D32.

## 2. Colour Palette
| Role              | Hex       | RGB            | Usage                              |
|-------------------|-----------|----------------|------------------------------------|
| Primary Green     | #2E7D32   | 46, 125, 50    | Logo, main CTAs, success states    |
| Accent Green      | #4CAF50   | 76, 175, 80    | Buttons, highlights, checkmarks    |
| Dark Green        | #1B5E20   | 27, 94, 32     | Hover states, darker accents       |
| Primary Blue      | #0D47A1   | 13, 71, 161    | Trust / compliance, secondary text |
| Secondary Blue    | #1976D2   | 25, 118, 210   | Links, secondary buttons           |
| Dark Text         | #212121   | 33, 33, 33     | Body text                          |
| Light Grey BG     | #F5F7FA   | 245, 247, 250  | Section backgrounds, cards         |
| White             | #FFFFFF   | 255, 255, 255  | Main background, text on dark      |

**Note**: Lean more into the green of the app icon for energy and “on the run” feel, while retaining blue for professionalism and SARS/compliance trust.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings**: System stack – ‘Segoe UI’, Roboto, -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, sans-serif. Bold / 700.
- **Body**: Same stack, regular / 400. Line-height 1.6.
- **Buttons**: Bold, slightly larger tracking if needed.
- Avoid decorative or script fonts.

## 4. Voice & Tone
- Professional yet approachable.
- Clear, benefit-focused, South African English.
- Emphasise: affordability, privacy (local storage), SARS & UIF readiness, simplicity.
- Avoid hype or unsubstantiated claims. Always keep the government disclaimer visible where relevant.

## 5. Key Messaging Pillars
1. **Affordable** – From R50 per employee slot.
2. **Private** – Data never leaves your device (POPIA-friendly).
3. **Compliant** – SARS tables, EMP201, UI19, uFiling CSV.
4. **Mobile-first** – Professional payroll in your pocket.
5. **Built for SA** – SMEs and household employers.

## 6. Imagery
- Prefer real app screenshots (phone mockups).
- Icons: simple, flat, in brand green or blue.
- Avoid stock photos of generic offices if possible; keep it product-focused.
