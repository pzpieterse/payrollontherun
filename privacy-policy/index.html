Privacy Policy

Effective as of: 5 October 2026 · Applies to the Payroll On The Run Android app and this website.

In short

  • Your payroll records (employer details, employees, ID numbers, salaries, bank details, leave, contracts, disciplinary records) are stored on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • Google Play version: we receive no personal data. Google handles your purchases under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Version downloaded from payrollontherun.com: to activate and check your licence, the app sends a small set of licence and device details (such as your e-mail address, a device ID and the device model) to our licence server. Card payments go through Paystack; we never see your card number.
  • No advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting tools are built into the app.
  • The app does not encrypt its own database. Protect your phone with a screen lock.

2. Who is responsible for which information

  • Payroll and employee information on your phone: you, as the employer, are the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). You decide what to enter and what to share. We have no access to it.
  • Licence and account information we receive (section 4.2, website version only): Pieter Pieterse (Payroll On The Run) is the responsible party.

3. What the app stores on your phone

Everything you enter is saved in the app’s private storage on your device (SQLite database files potr_<CODE>.db, one per employer, plus potr_config.db, and app settings):

  • Employer: name, registration/ID number, PAYE, UIF, SDL and COIDA numbers, address, telephone, e-mail, contact person, bargaining council details.
  • Employees: name, ID or passport number, nationality, date of birth, gender, telephone, e-mail, address, income tax number, payment method and bank details (bank, branch code, account number), pay day, a photo if you add one, termination date and reason.
  • Pay data: remuneration, payroll transactions (salary, PAYE, UIF, SDL, deductions), IRP5 codes, leave, contracts, disciplinary records and terminations.
  • Settings: app PIN, language, home-screen layout, your chosen documents folder and cached purchase/unlock status.

Reports and files. Payslips, EMP201/EMP501, UI-19, bargaining council returns, contracts, CSV exports and database backup copies are created on your device, in a folder you choose, only when you tap the button. They stay there until you move or share them.

4. What is sent off your phone, and why

There are two versions of the app. What leaves your phone depends on where you installed it.

4.1 Google Play version

  • We receive no personal data. The Google Play version does not contact our licence server.
  • Subscriptions are bought through Google Play Billing. Google collects your payment details directly, under Google’s privacy policy. The app reads which subscriptions are active from Google on your phone and keeps that status on the phone.
  • The Google Play Billing library built into the app may send technical diagnostic data about the billing connection (such as success or failure of billing calls) to Google, over an encrypted connection, under Google’s privacy policy. We do not receive this data. It contains no payroll or employee data.

5. Sharing that you choose

  • E-mail, WhatsApp, Share and Print: when you tap these on a report or payslip, the file goes to the app or printer you pick. From then on that app’s or service’s own terms apply.
  • Links: tapping a link opens your browser at payrollontherun.com (including the contact page), sars.gov.za, ufiling.labour.gov.za, ccma.org.za, gov.za, mibfa.co.za or patax.co.za. Those sites have their own privacy policies.
  • We do not sell personal information and do not share it for advertising.

6. Android backup

The app allows Android’s backup. If backup is on in your phone’s settings, the app’s private data, including the payroll databases, may be copied to your own Google account backup or to a new phone during transfer. This is controlled by your Google account and phone settings, not by us; we cannot see these backups. You can switch backup off in your phone’s settings.

7. Where the server data is kept (cross-border transfer)

Our licence server runs on Supabase in the eu-central-1 region (Frankfurt, Germany). Licence and account information from the website version is therefore transferred outside South Africa. (The Google Play version sends us nothing.) This transfer is allowed under section 72 of POPIA because (a) Germany is subject to the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), which gives protection substantially similar to POPIA (section 72(1)(a)), and (b) the transfer is necessary to perform our licence agreement with you (section 72(1)(c)).

8. Security

  • Data sent between the app and our server, Google Play or Paystack travels over encrypted HTTPS connections.
  • On your phone the app does not add its own encryption. Its database and exported files (including backup copies) are stored in plain form and rely on Android’s device encryption. Use a screen lock and the app PIN, keep your phone updated, and store exported files safely.
  • Supabase publicly states that customer data on its platform is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS) (supabase.com/security).

9. How long we keep information

  • On your phone: until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app. Files you exported stay until you delete them.
  • Google Play version: we hold no personal data about you.
  • On our licence server (website version): licence information is kept while your licence is active and for 12 months after that.
  • After a deletion request: we delete your licence record and keep only the payment records the law requires (for example Paystack payment records for SARS purposes), for 5 years.

10. Deleting your data

  • On your phone: Setup > Delete All My Data (Account Deletion) clears all the app’s data on the device, including employees, transactions, settings and licence settings. PDFs and exports you already saved are not removed; delete them yourself. Setup also links to our delete-account page.
  • On our server (website version): the app cannot delete your licence record itself. Ask us by e-mail at support@payrollontherun.com; we respond within 30 days. See payrollontherun.com/delete-account.

11. Your rights under POPIA

You may ask us whether we hold personal information about you and for a copy of it, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to its processing. Contact support@payrollontherun.com. We will respond within 30 days. To protect your information, we only delete data when the request comes from the e-mail address on the licence, or after you confirm the request from that address.

Employees: your employer keeps your payroll information on their own device, and we have no access to it. Please direct requests about that information to your employer.

Verslae en lêers. Betaalstrokies, EMP201/EMP501, UI-19, bedingingsraadopgawes, kontrakte, CSV-uitvoere en rugsteunkopieë van die databasis word op jou toestel geskep, in ’n vouer wat jy kies, en net wanneer jy die knoppie tik. Hulle bly daar totdat jy dit skuif of deel.

Ons lisensiebediener loop op Supabase in die eu-central-1-streek (Frankfurt, Duitsland). Lisensie- en rekeninginligting van die webwerf-weergawe word dus buite Suid-Afrika oorgedra. Artikel 72 van POPIA laat hierdie oordrag toe omdat (a) Duitsland onder die EU se Algemene Databeskermingsregulasie (AVG/GDPR) val, wat beskerming bied wat wesenlik soortgelyk aan POPIA is (artikel 72(1)(a)), en (b) die oordrag nodig is om ons lisensie-ooreenkoms met jou uit te voer (artikel 72(1)(c)).

8. Sekuriteit

  • Data wat tussen die app en ons bediener, Google Play of Paystack beweeg, gaan oor geënkripteerde HTTPS-verbindings.

  • Supabase verklaar openbaar dat kliëntdata op sy platform in rus (AES-256) en in transito (TLS) geënkripteer word (supabase.com/security).
