# Payroll On The Run – Website Layout & Implementation Brief
**August 2026**

## Overall Goals
- Increase trust and conversions from organic traffic and Facebook ads.
- Align visual identity with the Play Store app (green energy + professional blue).
- Make the privacy / local-storage benefit highly visible.
- Provide a clean, high-converting destination for paid traffic.

## Pages Required
1. **Homepage** (`/`) – full marketing site
2. **Ad Landing Page** (`/get-started` or `/download`) – minimal, conversion-focused

## Homepage Structure (top to bottom)
1. Sticky white header
   - Logo (Play Store green icon) left
   - Nav links: Features | How it Works | Pricing | Privacy
   - Green “Get the App” button right
2. Hero (blue-to-green gradient or solid blue with green accents)
   - Large headline + sub-headline
   - Primary green CTA + secondary text link
   - 2–3 phone mockups (app screenshots) on the right or below on mobile
3. Features grid (3–6 cards)
4. How it Works (3 numbered steps)
5. Who It’s For (simple icons or short list)
6. Pricing block (dark background, green price highlight)
7. Short FAQ accordion or simple list
8. Final CTA strip
9. Footer with support email + disclaimer

## Ad Landing Page Structure
- Minimal header (logo + “Back to site” optional)
- Hero with matching ad headline
- 3–4 benefit bullets with green checkmarks
- Large green “Get it on Google Play” button
- 1–2 phone screenshots
- Trust / disclaimer line
- Support contact
- No heavy navigation

## Technical Notes
- Mobile-first responsive
- Fast loading (optimise images, minimal JS)
- Use the colour palette from Branding Guidelines
- Add Open Graph / Twitter meta tags for better Facebook sharing
- Include the official Play Store badge / deep link
- Keep the existing privacy policy content (update date if needed)

## Assets Needed from You
- High-resolution version of the official app icon (green OnTheRun logo)
- 3–5 clean screenshots from the app (or use the ones already on Play Store)
- Current free-trial wording (2 or 3 months) so pricing copy is accurate
