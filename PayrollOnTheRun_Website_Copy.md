### FAQ (short version)
**Is this affiliated with SARS?**  
No. Payroll On The Run is an independent software application. It is not affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by SARS, the Department of Employment and Labour, or the South African Government.

**Where is my data stored?**  
Only on your device. We do not host, transmit or store your employee data on our servers.

**Does it work offline?**  
Core calculations and records work on your device. You only need internet to download the app or update tax tables if required.

**Can I use it for domestic workers?**  
Yes. It is designed for both small businesses and household employers.
