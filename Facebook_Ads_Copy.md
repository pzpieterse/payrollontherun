## General Tips
- Always direct traffic to the dedicated `/get-started` page (not the full homepage) for better conversion tracking.
- Use the green app icon or phone screenshots as creative.
- Include the disclaimer in the ad or on the landing page (already present).
- Test both “R50” and “First employee free” as the main hook.
- Audience suggestions: small business owners, interest in SARS / UIF / payroll / domestic workers, South Africa geo.
