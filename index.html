Frequently Asked Questions

Is this affiliated with SARS or the government?

No. Payroll On The Run is an independent software application. It is not affiliated with, authorised by, or endorsed by SARS, the Department of Employment and Labour, or the South African Government.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. We do not host, transmit or store your employee data on our servers. This gives you full control and strong POPIA alignment.

Can I use it for domestic workers?

Yes. It is designed for both small businesses and household employers who need simple, compliant payroll and UIF records.

Do I need internet every time?

Core calculations and records work on your device. You mainly need internet to download the app or receive updates.

Privacy & Data Safety

Local Storage Only model. All data (employee names, ID numbers, salaries, tax records) is stored exclusively on your device’s internal encrypted storage. We do not host or transmit your data to our servers.

You remain the responsible party under POPIA. Banking details entered stay on your phone and are only used to generate payslip records.
